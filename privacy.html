Privacy policy

Estudio de canales · last updated 5 November 2026

Who uses this app

Estudio de canales is a personal tool used only by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What Google data it accesses

  • Basic information about the connected YouTube channel (name, handle, picture, banner, description).
  • The channel's videos and their details (titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status, schedule, statistics).
  • YouTube Analytics reports for the channel (views, watch time, retention, subscribers, estimated revenue).
  • Permission to upload and update videos, thumbnails, captions and channel details on that channel.

How the data is used

Only to do what the owner asks inside the app: upload and schedule his own videos, update their texts and thumbnails, update his channel's details, and show his channels' statistics so he can compare videos and improve future ones. The data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone.

Where the data is stored

Everything stays on the owner's own computer. The access tokens are stored in a private folder readable only by his user account; the statistics are saved as local files. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

How to remove access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account page (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Disconnecting a channel inside the app also deletes its local token.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Estudio de canales' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Política de privacidad

Estudio de canales es una herramienta personal: sólo la usa su dueño, para sus propios canales de YouTube. Accede a la información del canal conectado, a sus vídeos, a sus estadísticas de YouTube Analytics y al permiso de subir y editar vídeos, miniaturas, subtítulos y los datos del canal. Lo usa únicamente para lo que el dueño pide en la app: subir y programar sus vídeos, actualizar sus textos y miniaturas, y ver estadísticas para mejorar. Nunca se vende, se comparte ni se usa para publicidad. Todo se guarda en su propio ordenador. El acceso se puede retirar cuando se quiera en myaccount.google.com/permissions. El uso de la información de las APIs de Google cumple la Política de datos de usuario de los servicios de API de Google, incluidos los requisitos de uso limitado.
